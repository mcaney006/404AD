! Title: 404AD YouTube Adapter
! Description: Network and cosmetic rules paired with the YouTube runtime
! adapter in packages/extension/src/adapters/youtube.ts.
!
! Division of labour, and the reason this list is not simply longer:
!
!   * Anything that is a plain request or a plain selector lives here.
!   * Anything that requires reading or rewriting the player's own data model
!     lives in the adapter, because YouTube does not deliver video ads as
!     separate blockable requests. The ad manifest arrives inside the same
!     /youtubei/v1/player response that carries playback configuration.
!
! That is why /youtubei/v1/player is NOT blocked below. Blocking it does not
! remove the ad, it removes the video.
!
! Selectors use native :has(), which Chromium has supported since 105. The
! compiler leaves those in the injected stylesheet rather than routing them
! through the JS procedural engine.

! NETWORK

! --- Telemetry beacons -----------------------------------------------------
! Quality-of-experience and CSI beacons. The player does not read the response,
! so these never gate playback.
||youtube.com/api/stats/qoe$xmlhttprequest
||youtube.com/csi_204$xmlhttprequest
||youtube.com/generate_204$xmlhttprequest
||youtube.com/error_204$xmlhttprequest
||youtube.com/gen_204$xmlhttprequest
||youtube.com/youtubei/v1/feedback$xmlhttprequest

! --- Exceptions ------------------------------------------------------------
! The player reads ad_status.js to decide whether an ad slot resolved. Letting
! it 200 is what keeps the adapter's payload stripping from looking to the
! player like a stalled ad request.
@@||doubleclick.net/instream/ad_status.js$script,domain=youtube.com|youtube-nocookie.com
! Playback and resume position both ride on watchtime; blocking it loses your
! place in long videos.
@@||youtube.com/api/stats/watchtime$xmlhttprequest

! ===========================================================================
! SCRIPTLETS
! ===========================================================================
! The runtime adapter. Strips ad payloads out of player responses and feed data,
! drives the player state machine, and dismisses the enforcement modal.
youtube.com##+js(404ad-yt-player)
youtube-nocookie.com##+js(404ad-yt-player)

! The SABR/UMP transport engine. Loads its own WASM module lazily, on the first
! media request, so a YouTube page that never starts playback never pays for it.
youtube.com##+js(404ad-yt-transport)
youtube-nocookie.com##+js(404ad-yt-transport)

! Belt and braces for the inlined bootstrap objects, in case the adapter's
! property hook loses the race with an inline script.
youtube.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.adPlacements, undefined)
youtube.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.playerAds, undefined)
youtube.com##+js(set-constant, ytInitialPlayerResponse.adSlots, undefined)
youtube.com##+js(set-constant, playerResponse.adPlacements, undefined)

! Drop the instrumentation timers the player installs for ad pacing.
youtube.com##+js(json-prune, adPlacements, playerAds, adSlots, adBreakHeartbeatParams)
